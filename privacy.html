Kalmvue

Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Kalmvue shows the newest videos from the YouTube channels you subscribe to. It is run by Houston. Google and YouTube do not make it.

What you sign in to

Google handles sign-in. Kalmvue asks only for read-only access to your YouTube account, the permission called youtube.readonly. With that permission it reads:

  • the list of channels you subscribe to
  • the titles, thumbnails, and publish times of recent videos on those channels

Kalmvue cannot post, comment, like, subscribe, unsubscribe, or change your account. It never receives your Google password.

Where information is kept

The sign-in token stays in this browser tab and expires after about an hour. Your watched list, and the Hide watched and Show Shorts settings, are saved in this browser only. Kalmvue has no account database. Sign out and clear data, or clear this site's data in your browser, and that information is removed.

Videos open on YouTube. From then on, YouTube's privacy policy applies.

What this site does not do

Kalmvue does not sell information. It does not show its own ads. It does not use analytics or tracking cookies. The only outside script it loads is Google's sign-in script, and it loads that only when sign-in is turned on.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Render. Render may keep ordinary connection logs, such as a browser address and the page requested, in order to run the service. Kalmvue does not use those logs for advertising.

How to disconnect

Use Sign out and clear data in the menu. To remove Kalmvue's permission from your Google account, open Google account permissions and remove Kalmvue.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
