    About FixFocus

    FixFocus is a free way to search and watch repair, home, car, and DIY YouTube videos without Shorts, homepage feeds, or algorithmic recommendations. Type what is broken. FixFocus asks YouTube for matches, keeps the repair and how-to videos, and plays them here so you can finish the job and leave.

    Quick answers

    How do I watch YouTube repair videos without distractions or Shorts?
    Search here for the repair you need. FixFocus drops Shorts and plays the video on this page, so YouTube's homepage and suggested videos stay out of the way.
    Is FixFocus free?
    Yes. FixFocus is free to use. You do not pay to search or watch.
    Do I need an account?
    No. You can search and watch without signing in. Email updates are optional and are not an account.
    Does FixFocus host the videos?
    No. Videos play through YouTube. FixFocus is not made by, or affiliated with, YouTube or Google.
